METHOD & LIMITATIONS
01 / THE PIPELINE

A transparent stack.

  1. Pretrained perception. Google's MediaPipe Hand Landmarker locates 21 hand points. No claim of training this model.
  2. Geometry. Mean adjacent-joint angles estimate extension. Thumb–index distance is normalized by wrist-to-middle-MCP length.
  3. Temporal logic. A dwell-based state machine counts open-to-closed cycles with hysteresis.
  4. Personal learning. A kNN classifier (k=5) learns labeled six-dimensional examples collected here.
  5. Evaluation. Leave-one-session-out predictions are compared with a nearest-centroid baseline.
02 / WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Useful descriptors. Limited claims.

Openness is a geometric proxy, not a joint-angle measurement or a clinical assessment. World landmarks are used when available; screen landmarks are a fallback.

The cycle counter requires a stable open state followed by a stable closed state. Tracking loss resets its pending state to avoid phantom repetitions.

Calibration maps your comfortable extremes to 0–100. Without it, the interface uses documented geometric defaults. The chart and session export preserve whether input was simulated or camera-derived.

03 / EVALUATION PROTOCOL

Keep sessions apart.

Adjacent video frames are highly correlated. Random frame splitting can inflate accuracy. Every test session is excluded entirely from its training fold.

Report macro F1, accuracy, a confusion matrix and the centroid baseline. Results remain estimates on your own small dataset; they do not establish performance on new people.

A stronger follow-up: collect multiple participants and hold out each participant entirely, then repeat under changed lighting and backgrounds.

04 / KNOWN FAILURE MODES

Show where it breaks.

Occluded fingers, motion blur, partial hands, side views and poor lighting can degrade tracking. Unusual hand postures may fall outside the pretrained model's coverage.

By default the classifier picks a known class. Optional held-session distance rejection can reject unfamiliar inputs, but has not been validated on webcam data. Vote agreement is not proof of correctness. No patient study or therapeutic benefit has been established.
